Deficiency Checklist

Why no energy and what to do about it?

Tired in the morning?

Possible deficiency of iron (ferritin) or vitamin D. Check your vitamin D level (optimal range: 60-80 ng/ml).

Sugar cravings?

This is often a signal of chromium or magnesium deficiency. Magnesium is depleted first during stress.

Poor sleep?

Try adding magnesium citrate or glycinate (400 mg) an hour before bed. This helps the nervous system relax.
