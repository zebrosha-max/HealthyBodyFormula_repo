Hormonal Glow:
Cycle-Synced Nutrition

By phases 100% Feminine

Your cycle is your superpower. Throughout the month, a woman's hormonal profile constantly changes, affecting metabolic rate, brain function, and even how efficiently the body absorbs proteins and fats.

This protocol will teach you not to fight your body, but to support it in each phase, using nutrients as a fine-tuning tool for well-being and beauty.

Part 1: Biochemistry

The Estrogen and Progesterone Dance

In the first phase, estrogen dominates — the hormone of energy and radiance. In the second (after ovulation), progesterone takes the stage. It raises body temperature and basal metabolism by 200-300 kcal, but also reduces insulin sensitivity, which causes those sweet cravings before menstruation.

Part 2: Four Phases — Four Strategies

We divide nutrition into stages corresponding to the body's physiological needs.

1. Follicular Phase (Spring)

Period: Day 1 to 12 of the cycle.

Goal: Support follicle maturation and prepare the liver for eliminating used hormones.

Protocol:

  • Cruciferous: Broccoli and cauliflower contain DIM, which helps the liver safely metabolize estrogens.
  • Fermented foods: For a healthy estrobolome (microflora that affects estrogen).
  • Light proteins: Poultry, lean fish, sprouts.
2. Ovulatory Phase (Summer)

Period: Days 13-16 (mid-cycle).

Goal: Energy is at its peak. Control inflammation during egg release.

Protocol:

  • Fiber: Maximum vegetables and greens to eliminate excess estrogen.
  • Antioxidants: Berries, green tea, turmeric.
  • Moderation: You may feel less hungry — listen to your body.
3. Luteal Phase (Autumn)

Period: From day 17 to the start of menstruation.

Goal: Support progesterone, control appetite, and fight bloating.

Protocol:

  • Magnesium: Dark chocolate (85%+), pumpkin seeds, almonds. Magnesium relieves PMS and sugar cravings.
  • Complex carbs: Brown rice, sweet potato, buckwheat. They help synthesize serotonin, preventing mood swings.
  • Healthy fats: Avocado, pine nuts — building material for progesterone.
4. Menstrual Phase (Winter)

Goal: Replenish deficiencies and warm the body.

Protocol:

  • Iron: Organ meats, red meat (if you consume it), lentils, spinach.
  • Warm food: Soups, stews, warming spices (ginger, cinnamon).
  • Vitamin C: Rose hip, kiwi — for better iron absorption.

Part 3: Troubleshooting

1. Bloating in the Second Phase

Cause: Sodium retention under the influence of progesterone or electrolyte imbalance.

Solution: Minimize salt 5 days before your cycle. Drink herbal teas (nettle, dandelion) and add potassium (parsley, dried apricots, baked potato).

2. Sugar Cravings Before Period

Solution: Increase overall caloric intake by 150-200 kcal through fats and proteins at lunch. When the brain is well-nourished, it sends fewer SOS signals in the form of chocolate cravings.

Safety Criteria

Important: This protocol is supportive. If you have amenorrhea (absence of cycle), severe pain (suspected endometriosis), or ovarian cysts — you must get examined by a gynecologist-endocrinologist.
