Where to Start Your Health Journey?

A basic checklist of habits and simple steps for a gentle start

The main rule: don't try to change everything at once. Pick 2-3 items and introduce them gradually. Sustainable habits form in 21-66 days.

Step 1: Water

1 Start your day with a glass of water

Drink a glass of warm water (200-300 ml) right after waking up, before breakfast. This kickstarts your metabolism and helps your body wake up.

2 Daily water intake

Target: 30 ml per 1 kg of body weight. At 60 kg that's ~1.8 liters. Tea and coffee don't count. Carry a bottle — visual reminders work best.

Step 2: Nutrition

3 Eat when hungry, not by the clock

Listen to your body. Real hunger builds gradually, not suddenly. If you crave "something tasty" — it's likely emotional hunger.

4 Add vegetables to every meal

Don't remove favorite foods — just add vegetables. Salad with pasta, cucumber with a sandwich, greens in an omelet. This naturally increases fiber and vitamins.

5 Don't skip breakfast

Breakfast with protein and complex carbs (eggs + whole grain bread, cottage cheese + berries) stabilizes blood sugar and reduces sugar cravings during the day.

Step 3: Sleep

6 Go to bed before 11 PM

Melatonin is produced between 10 PM and 2 AM. The earlier you fall asleep in this window, the better your rest. Put screens away an hour before bed.

7 7-8 hours minimum

Sleep deprivation raises cortisol, increases appetite, and slows metabolism. Sleep is not a luxury — it's a basic need.

Step 4: Movement

8 8,000 steps a day

No need to hit the gym. A walk after lunch, stairs instead of elevator, standing phone calls — small habits add up to big results.

Step 5: Mindfulness

9 Keep a food diary

For at least the first 2 weeks, write down everything you eat. Not to count calories — but to see your real habits. Use our bot — just send a food photo!

10 Don't blame yourself for slip-ups

Had cake? That's okay. One day doesn't define results. What matters is the overall trend, not every single meal. Get back to your plan without guilt.

💡 Tip: Pick 2-3 easiest items from this list. Do them for 3 weeks. When it becomes easy — add one more. This way you'll build a foundation of healthy habits without stress.
