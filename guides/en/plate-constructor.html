The 50/25/25 Method: Perfect balance without scales

This method is used by Harvard nutritionists. It allows you to feel satisfied, lose weight, and maintain energy levels without counting calories.

The Golden Formula

  • 50% — Fiber: Vegetables (except potatoes), greens. This is your satiety and a healthy microbiome.
  • 25% — Protein: Poultry, fish, eggs, legumes, or seafood. This is building material and appetite control.
  • 25% — Complex Carbs: Buckwheat, brown rice, quinoa, whole grain bread. This is energy for your brain and muscles.

Practical Tip

Always start your meal with the vegetable portion. Fiber creates a "net" in the intestines that slows down sugar absorption and prevents insulin spikes. This will save you from post-lunch drowsiness.
